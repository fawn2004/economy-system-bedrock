SKYBLOCK ECONOMY v1.0.61 - EVERYTHING IN THE ADD-ON
=====================================================

PACKS
- Behaviour pack + Resource pack (no experiments needed, Minecraft 1.21.90+)
- Chat Ranks pack (optional, needs Beta APIs) - puts ranks in front of chat messages
- Admins: /tag <name> add admin   (gives the red ADMIN tile in the menu)

HOW PLAYERS USE IT
- Menu Compass: given on join, kept on death, can't be dropped. Use it to open the menu.
- NPCs: big shopkeepers you tap instead of using the compass (Shop, Sell, Menu, Bazaar,
  Auction House, Banker, Island, Home, Crates, Ranks). Admins place them in Admin > NPCs.
- All menus look like Hypixel-style chests: item slots, glass panes, hover tooltips,
  Go Back / Close / page arrows / Search on the bottom row.
- Sidebar on the right (grey with a gold border): date, players online, season & time,
  location, rank, purse, bank and your current objective. Full or Compact in Settings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
MONEY & TRADING
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHOP        Every vanilla item (~1,480) in categories + Search. Kits, Spawners,
            Minions, Chunk Hopper, Skill Tools, Equipment, Pets.
            Prices are worked out so buying -> crafting -> selling never makes a profit.
SELL        Sell one item type or Sell All.
BAZAAR      Player market for materials (Farming, Mining, Combat, Blocks, Enchanted):
            instant buy/sell, buy orders, sell offers, claim/cancel. 1% tax.
AUCTIONS    Sell items from your inventory as BIN or bidding auctions (1h-48h).
            Browse by type, search, sort. Pays offline players when they join.
BANK        Safe account with 2% interest every 6 hours. 5 account tiers
            (Basic -> Diamond). Deposit / withdraw / history.
PAY         Send money to online players.
RANKINGS    Top 10 leaderboards: skill average, each skill, balance, heads, farming.
MONEY       Balances up to 1 quadrillion; shown as $2.4M / $2.4B etc.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR ISLAND (NEW)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tap the Island NPC -> a small grass island in the void with a tree + starter chest.
- One island per player, islands are 1000 blocks apart.
- Size: 100x100 -> 200x200 ($500k) -> 300x300 ($2.5M).
- Border pushes you back (green particles near the edge). Void = teleport home.
- Only you + your members can build / open chests. Visitors on/off.
- Menu > Island: Home, Set Home, Upgrade, Members, Visitors, Visit, Show Border, Hub.
- Home NPC teleports you back to your island.
- Admin > Islands: set Hub, delete a player's island, teleport to islands.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
CRATES (NEW)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 5 crates: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic - each with its own model,
  colours, floating gem and sparkles.
- Hold the right key and tap: lid opens, rewards spin on screen, you win one.
  Big wins are announced in chat.
- Tap without a key: see every reward and its % chance, buy keys.
- Rewards: money pouches (bigger each tier), tools, custom items, spawners, pets,
  rank vouchers, higher crate keys.
- Keys: Menu > Crates (buy Common/Rare/Epic), Daily Reward (Common, Epic every 7th
  day), dungeons, slayer bosses, other crates.
- Admin > Crates: place / remove crates, give keys, make money pouches.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAT RANKS (NEW)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 112 ranks + 8 staff ranks (Owner, Co-Owner, Admin, Mod, Helper, Build Team,
  YouTube, Streamer).
- Colour styles: solid colours, rainbow, fire, ice, galaxy, toxic, royal, ocean,
  sunset, candy, lava and more.
- Rarities: Common $25k, Rare $150k, Epic $750k, Legendary $3M, Mythic (crates only).
- Shows in chat, above your head and on the sidebar. Menu > Ranks to buy / switch.
- Rank Vouchers from crates; Admin > Ranks gives anyone any rank.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATS, ITEMS & GEAR
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATS       Health, Defense, Strength, Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Speed, Intelligence,
            Magic Find, Mining Speed, Mining/Farming/Foraging Fortune, Sea Creature
            Chance. Profile > Stats explains each one and where it comes from.
ITEMS       ~80 custom items with rarities (Common -> Mythic): shop equipment, slayer
            gear, dungeon gear, boss materials. Some have right-click abilities that
            use mana (teleport, fire bolt, heal party, shockwave...).
REFORGE     Profile > Reforge: random reforge on weapons, armor and tools.
ENCHANTING  Profile > Enchanting: custom enchants up to XV that really work -
            Keen Edge, Life Drain, Finisher, Precision, Venom, Storm Call, Scavenger,
            Vitality, Bulwark, Fleetfoot, Insight, Regrowth, Retaliate, Last Stand,
            Nightsight, Harvesting, Swiftswing, Lure Master.
            Each attempt has a % chance; a fail still costs.
            Every hit shows a combat line, e.g. "19 dmg | Keen +40% | Storm +75%".
WARDROBE    9 saved armor sets, swap in one tap.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SKILLS & PROGRESS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SKILLS      Farming, Mining, Foraging, Excavating, Fishing, Combat (max 25).
            Each level: perk +2% and a coin reward you claim in the Skills menu.
MY PROGRESS Skill average, rank, weakest skills, closest collections, next unlocks.
COLLECTIONS Every crop and spawner mob: 5 tiers, pays money + bonus drop chance.
SKILL TOOLS Farming Hoe & Slayer Sword: wooden, unbreakable, levels 1-15, show their
            kills / crops. Upgrades (Sharpness, Looting, Fortune, Replenish...) paid
            with money + heads / enchanted crops. Work together with kit armor.
KITS        6 upgradable kit lines (Farming, Mining, Foraging, Fishing, Combat,
            Dungeon), levels 1-10, claim every 12h. Better materials, enchants,
            reforges and rarity each level. Preview shows every item.
PETS        Wolf, Rabbit, Bee, Silverfish, Fox, Dolphin, Allay, Bat, Drakeling.
            Level 1-100, upgrade rarity, one follows you at a time, trade as eggs.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMBAT CONTENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLAYER      4 bosses (Rotting Tyrant, Brood Mother, Bone Colossus, Void Stalker),
            5 tiers, slayer levels 1-9, boss drops + Slayer Shop gear.
DUNGEONS    Party runs (up to 5), Floors I-V: monster, elite, puzzle and boss rooms,
            secret chests, score grades S+ to D, classes (Warrior, Mage, Ranger,
            Guardian, Cleric), Essence shop, item stars, reward chests with
            dungeon-only tools and treasures to sell, spirits.
SEA         Fishing can pull up tough sea creatures.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAILY STUFF
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
DAILY       Claim every 12h, grows with your streak (+ crate key). Every 7th day bonus.
QUESTS      3 daily commissions + 17 one-time milestones.
ELECTION    Vote for a mayor every 3 days; the mayor's perks help everyone.
SPIRITS     Rare dungeon drops; every 5 = permanent stats.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISLAND BUILDINGS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPAWNERS    24 mobs. Stack up to 16, upgrade levels 1-10 with enchanted heads,

MINIONS     5 mining + 5 farming minions ($25k -> $50M) with Speed, Luck, Fortune,
            Storage and Better Blocks upgrades. Work while you're away.
CHUNK HOPPER Collects every dropped item in its chunk; Sell All / Collect;
            storage + speed upgrades.
