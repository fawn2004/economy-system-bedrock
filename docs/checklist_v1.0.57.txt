SKILL TOOL TOOLTIPS
[ ] Hovering the Slayer Sword shows "Kills with this sword: X" and it goes up when you kill mobs
[ ] Hovering the Farming Hoe shows "Crops harvested: X" and it goes up when you harvest grown crops
[ ] The tooltip shows the next level that needs kills / crops, e.g. "Lv 4: 1,000 kills (you: 523)"

LEVEL 15
[ ] Skill tools level up to 15 (tooltip says Level x/15)
[ ] Every upgrade (except Replenish, which is on/off) goes to level 15
[ ] Upgrade costs rise each level
[ ] Sharpness above V: the sword keeps Sharpness V and the extra levels give +6% damage each
[ ] Looting above III gives +5% bonus drops per level
[ ] Fire Aspect above II gives +3% damage per level
[ ] Fortune above III (hoe) gives +5% bonus crops per level
[ ] Efficiency above V (hoe) gives +1% Enchanted crop chance per level
[ ] The upgrade screen explains what the levels above the vanilla maximum do

KIT ARMOR + SKILL TOOLS
[ ] Wearing Combat Kit armor while using the Slayer Sword gives more damage and more bonus drops
[ ] Sometimes a kill with the sword + kit armor gives a random chunk of coins ("+$X (kit bonus)")
[ ] The sword tooltip shows the kit bonus (e.g. "Kit: +24% dmg, +32% drops") or "Wear Combat Kit armor for a bonus"
[ ] More kit pieces and a higher kit level = bigger bonus
[ ] Dungeon Kit armor also counts for the Slayer Sword
[ ] Farming Kit now gives Harvester armor from level 1
[ ] Wearing Farming Kit (Harvester) armor with the Farming Hoe gives more crops, more hoe XP and sometimes coins
[ ] The hoe tooltip shows its kit bonus
